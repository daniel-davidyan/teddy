Teddy HQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Teddy HQ is a private, single-user content-management tool operated by the owner of the Instagram account @teddy.the.golden1 ("Teddy The Golden") to manage that account only. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

  • Profile information, media, insights and comments of the owner's own Instagram professional account, via the official Instagram Graph API with the owner's consent.
  • Public information about public Business/Creator accounts returned by the official Business Discovery API (follower counts and public post metrics).
  • Text of public comments left on the owner's own posts, used only to draft replies that the owner reviews and approves.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted where sensitive (access tokens), and is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Nothing is published automatically: every post, reply or comment is explicitly approved by the owner.
  • Data is kept only as long as needed to operate the tool and can be deleted at any time by the owner.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to you (for example, a comment you left on @teddy.the.golden1), send a direct message to @teddy.the.golden1 on Instagram. Removing the app from your Facebook/Instagram settings revokes its access immediately.
